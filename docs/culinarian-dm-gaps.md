# Culinarian — DM Rules & Open Questions

This file is the implementation's source of truth for what is known versus what still needs a DM ruling. Unknown mechanics are intentionally not invented.

## Confirmed class details
- Hit Die: d8.
- Spellcasting ability: Intelligence.
- Spell-slot progression: full caster, through 9th-level slots.
- Armor proficiency: light and medium armor.
- Tool proficiency: Cook's Utensils.
- Recipes function as the class's spell-like abilities.

## Confirmed Confectioner notes currently represented in the app
- Level 3: Culinary Constructs; up to three active constructs, shared HP pool, Guardian/Medic/Saboteur/Striker recipes.
- Level 6: Magical Seasoning; spend a spell slot to add Spicy, Savory, or Bitter seasoning to a commanded construct ability.
- Level 10: Expanded Brigade; up to four constructs, improved shared HP, magical attacks, and a second command.
- Level 14: Grand Banquet; one-minute empowered brigade, up to five constructs, shared temporary HP, increased speed, and once-per-turn benefit.
- Grapevine healing construct is recorded as a proposed/working recipe and requires DM confirmation as an official fifth recipe.

## DM questions / unresolved gaps
1. What are the Culinarian's saving throw proficiencies?
2. How many skills does the class choose, and from what list?
3. What are the exact starting equipment options and weapon proficiencies?
4. What is the complete base-class feature progression at levels 1–20?
5. How many recipes are known at each level, and how are new recipes learned?
6. How many recipes can be prepared at once? Can they be changed on a short rest, long rest, or another schedule?
7. Are recipes cast using spell slots exactly like spells? Are cantrips included?
8. What spellcasting focus, components, and ritual-casting rules apply?
9. What base-class resources exist, and when do they recover?
10. Does the class use the normal ASI/feat levels?
11. Is Confectioner selected at level 3? Are there other subclasses?
12. Is Grapevine an approved fifth construct recipe?
13. Do constructs move on the Culinarian's turn or on a separate initiative immediately after it?
14. Does an uncommanded construct Dodge automatically? If so, is that an explicit exception to the no-independent-actions rule?
15. How does Guardian's reaction refresh, and can more than one Guardian protect the same creature?
16. At level 10, does shared HP become 6 × Culinarian level (replacing 5 + 5 × level)?
17. How is shared HP damage handled when an area effect hits multiple constructs?
18. Does a successful save against Spoiled Ingredients still prevent reactions?
19. Can the same construct be commanded more than once on a turn through Expanded Brigade, and how does this interact with seasoning?
20. What exactly triggers Grand Banquet's once-per-turn bonus, and does the bonus apply to healing as well as temporary HP?

## Implementation policy
- Do not silently assign default saves, skills, equipment, weapon proficiencies, or base-class features while the above remains unknown.
- Keep the character creator usable; let the player manually enter ability and combat stats.
- Keep open questions visible in the Features tab and in this document until the DM resolves them.
